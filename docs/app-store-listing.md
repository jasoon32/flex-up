## Description (English)

Flex Up is a fast, no-fuss gym log for people who actually train.

PLAN YOUR WEEK
• Build plans from common splits: Push, Pull, Legs, Upper, Lower, Full Body and more
• Pick from 180+ gym exercises, or type your own
• Schedule plans on days of the week. Today's workout is waiting on Home

LOG EVERY SET FAST
• Last time's weights are already filled in
• Type a weight once and it carries down to the next sets
• kg, lb or bodyweight per exercise, with per-hand dumbbell lifts
• Automatic rest timer with a sound when it's time to go again
• Optional RPE for every set

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• 🏆 when you beat a personal record, 🔥 when you match one
• Day streaks and a weekly overview
• Full history of every workout and set

SHARE IT
• Story cards, stats cards and animated stickers for WeChat, WhatsApp and Instagram
• A pixel-art lifter you dress up yourself, doing the lift you just did

YOUR DATA, YOUR CALL
• No ads, no tracking
• Delete your account and data from Settings at any time

## App Review information

- **Sign-in required:** Yes. Create a demo account in the app first, add a plan and one finished workout, then give Apple its email and password here.
- **Notes for the reviewer:**
  > Flex Up is a workout logger. Sign in with the demo account, open Plans to see a plan, tap "Start this workout now", tick a set, and tap Finish to see the celebration and share cards. Account deletion: Settings → Delete account.
