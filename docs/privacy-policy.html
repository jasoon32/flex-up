Flex Up Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Flex Up is a workout tracker. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and how you can delete it. In short: we keep only what the app needs to save your training, we don't sell it, and we don't track you.

What we store

DataWhy
Your email address and a securely hashed passwordTo create your account and sign you in.
Your workout plans, logged workouts and sets (exercise names, weights, reps, optional RPE, dates and times)To show your plans, history, personal records and streaks, on any device you sign in on.
Your lifter character (the nickname and outfit you choose)To show your character in the app and on share cards.

What stays on your phone

  • Photos. If you add a photo to a share card, it is used on your device to make the image and is never uploaded.
  • Settings such as language, default rest time and weight steps are saved only on the device.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking, and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.
  • We don't use the microphone, your location or your contacts.

Where your data is kept

Account and workout data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, on servers in Tokyo, Japan. Data is sent over encrypted connections, and each account can only read its own data.

Sharing

Share cards and stickers are created on your phone. They are only sent somewhere when you choose to share them, for example in WeChat or Instagram, and then that app's own privacy policy applies.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, plans, workouts and history straight away. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

Children

Flex Up is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

Flex Up 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 25 日

Flex Up 是一款训练记录应用。我们只保存应用运行所需的数据，不出售数据，也不追踪你。

我们保存的数据

  • 邮箱和加密后的密码：用于创建账号和登录。
  • 训练计划、训练记录和每组数据（动作名称、重量、次数、可选 RPE、日期和时间）：用于显示计划、历史、个人纪录和连续天数，并可在任何设备登录查看。
  • 你的角色（你设置的昵称和服装）：用于在应用和分享卡片中显示。

只保存在手机上的数据

  • 照片：添加到分享卡片的照片只在手机上用于生成图片，不会上传。
  • 设置（语言、默认休息时间、重量步进等）只保存在本机。

我们不会做的事

  • 没有广告、广告追踪、数据分析或追踪 SDK。
  • 不会出售或与他人共享你的数据用于营销。
  • 不使用麦克风、位置或通讯录。

数据存放位置

账号和训练数据存放在我们的数据库和登录服务商 Supabase 位于日本东京的服务器上，通过加密连接传输，每个账号只能读取自己的数据。

删除数据

在应用中前往设置 → 删除账号，即可立即永久删除你的账号、计划、训练和历史记录。你也可以发邮件给我们代为删除。

儿童

Flex Up 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集他们的数据。
